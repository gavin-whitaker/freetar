Install on iPhone
  1. Open this site in Safari using the https://…:8443 address.
  2. Trust the certificate once: download the Freetar certificate, then open Settings → Profile Downloaded → Install, and turn it on under Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings.
  3. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  4. Open Freetar from the home screen and allow the microphone when the tuner asks.

Songs you open are saved on the phone, so they still work without a connection.
